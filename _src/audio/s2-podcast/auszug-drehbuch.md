## Misstrauen und Fehler

- [science_0001] "I used AI models to look for: mistakes, synonyms, incorrect grammar, better explanations, etc."
- [science_0009] "I think that it is naturally difficult for human beings to follow rules down to the letter without making any small mistakes, and that is what this particular assignment expects."
- [science_0026] "I think an ethical use of AI as a researcher is to think of the AI tools as a "brainstorming partner" (this is how I tend to try to write instructions for any LLMs I use) that can come up with ideas (some of which may be wrong) at a much quicker than I individually can search and implement."
- [science_0037] "I am quite sceptical of using AI for research/ writing as for research, I worry it will miss out something crucial, so i prefer to do it myself. In terms of writing, although i know it can be helpful for formatting or sometimes editing, I like my style of writing and if I use AI I know it's not me."
- [science_0045] "It's also an area where mistakes can ruin days of work, so I wouldn't trust AI for any step-by-step lab instructions without cross-checking everything. The cost of error when using AI is too high."
- [science_0054] "A lot of the time AI would hallucinate answers to things, suggest structures which are chemically impossible (e.g. 5 bonds around carbon), or not interpret the question correctly."
- [science_0065] "If I ask for example, "give me references for this claim in the literature" I've found the answers given are often incorrect or not very good. AI is also very bad at searching through astronomical databases."
- [science_0075] "For anything that involves numbers or mathematical formulae, I always check these carefully by hand as I think this is an area where mistakes are more likely to slip through."
- [science_0083] "Of course, I make mistakes sometimes, but they've never been because of how a sentence reads. I also compare my work to other publications."
- [science_0099] "There are areas where the AI completely hallucinates potential solutions and doesn't help at all when developing solutions for these issues. It's much faster and a lot more accurate to continue using traditional approaches"
- [science_0108] "or cutting edge research The more of these factors are present in your prompt the more likely it is that the AI's code will contain mistakes."
- [science_0117] "At the end, I tried to get AIs to proof-read the final Latex document, for example -- which meant there were quite a few mistakes that the AI made, as it was not able to correctly examine the visual proofs."

## Sorge um Fachkompetenz

- [science_0005] "Having said that, I think AI is very useful for helping me write the tools to process my data, I just think the critical thinking should be done solely by me (or my coauthors)."
- [science_0024] "Another way I used it was to help me manage system things such as dependencies, the virtual environment, version control. For example."
- [science_0029] "I guess I could've used AI in help for designing the prototypes, but it was also highly dependent on materials / resources we already had available at my work, and it would've taken a lot of time to give AI all the context of what we were working with / the constraints from my environment and workplace."
- [science_0043] "For instance, I could upload 10 papers and say "which of these papers found that diatom distribution varies dependent on nutrients and what species seem to be indicators of polluted water?""
- [science_0048] "AI can offer valuable suggestions for model setup and data collection, but feature selection and hyper parameter tuning are dependent on the context of the data. AI often doesn't do a great job applying whatever knowledge it has about the subject matter of the data to these problems"
- [science_0077] "I have this powerful tool to help me work through complex calculations and check my reasoning, but I'm still doing the critical thinking and learning. It's enhanced my problem-solving process without replacing my need to truly understand the material."
- [science_0079] "The database search was often more time-consuming than using Google Scholar because it was keyword dependent. When ChatGPT got access to searching the internet, I immediately adopted it because it could provide links based on entire paragraphs describing my hypothesis or research ideas, instead of relying on keywords."
- [science_0087] "Also I would have AI explain things to me about QuTip, such as time dependent Hamiltonians, if it was fully explained in the QuTip manual. I also found AI helpful in writing with LaTeX as some of the commands can be confusing there."
- [science_0089] "A very general point would be an over-reliance on the tool's training data that could compromise the user's intended purpose and the integrity of their usage of the response when using GenAI tools, especially in academia. This is the likely cause of the generic ideas that I mentioned earlier."

- [science_0014] "I don't think so, at the moment I wouldn't trust AI to fully complete any tasks without needed to double check them."
