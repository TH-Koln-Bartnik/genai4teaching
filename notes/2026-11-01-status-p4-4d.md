## Candidate permanent notes
- Competence frameworks for AI literacy specify that learners must evaluate AI output but rarely specify how.
- AI Fluency is the only major AI competence framework that treats responsibility towards third parties as a competence of its own.
- Reading the four D as two loops separates a strategic decision made once per project from an operative cycle repeated in every task.
- Prompting covers only one of four competences needed to work with AI responsibly.
- The bottleneck stage of the 4D cycle shifts from delegation and checking for an AI assistant to behaviour description for AI tutors and simulators.
